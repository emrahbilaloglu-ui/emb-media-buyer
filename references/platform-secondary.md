# Secondary channels and programmatic

Apply the core evidence, economics, measurement, and execution policies to every channel. This file is a routing checklist, not a substitute for current official documentation or an account read.

## Microsoft Advertising

Inspect account/currency/time zone, campaign type, networks/partners, search queries, brand/non-brand, match and negative controls, audiences, locations, devices, schedules, bids/budgets, conversion goals/UET, offline outcomes, shopping feed/catalog, asset/URL binding, attribution, and change history.

Do not transfer a Google Ads configuration or recommendation without verifying Microsoft’s current fields, inventory, audience availability, partner traffic, and conversion definitions.

## LinkedIn Ads

Inspect objective, account/currency/time zone, campaign-group/campaign/creative hierarchy, audience definitions and estimated/delivered composition, exclusions, placements/network, bid/budget, frequency, lead forms, website/Insight Tag, Conversions API or current server integration, CRM/offline stages, attribution, creative binding, and lead quality.

For B2B, optimize through qualified opportunity, pipeline, closed-won contribution, and sales-cycle lag. Job-title/company targeting labels are not proof of the exact people reached; platform demographic reporting is modeled/aggregated and must be qualified.

## Pinterest Ads

Inspect business/ad account, objective, campaign/ad-group/ad structure, keywords/interests/actalikes or current equivalents, shopping catalogs/product groups, Pin/creative and destination binding, conversion tag/API, attribution, placements, market, budgets/bids, and audience/creative reporting.

Separate inspiration/assisted behavior from incremental purchase. Use advertiser-owned commerce outcomes and current Pinterest measurement options.

## Snapchat Ads

Inspect objective, pixel/CAPI/app measurement, attribution, audiences, placements, formats, creative/destination binding, bids/budgets, delivery, and lift/MMM support. For app work, reconcile MMP/SKAN and retained cohort outcomes. View and swipe metrics are diagnostics, not contribution.

## Reddit Ads

Inspect account, campaign/ad group/ad, community/interest/keyword targeting or current equivalents, placement/device, creative/post identity, destination, Pixel and Conversions API, event-id/deduplication, attribution, bids/budgets, comments/brand risk, conversion quality, and advertiser-owned outcomes.

The community context can affect interpretation and brand safety; do not copy organic engagement logic into a commercial target without measurement.

## Amazon Ads

Separate Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, DSP, and current formats. Inspect marketplace, seller/vendor account, catalog/ASIN, retail readiness, Buy Box/availability, price/promotion, search term/target, placement, audience, creative/store destination, bids/budgets, attributed sales window, new-to-brand, total retail sales, margin, and inventory.

Attributed ad sales, TACoS, ACOS, and new-to-brand answer different questions. Retail halo and organic rank can matter but require a design or qualified analysis. Do not increase spend into stock, margin, or Buy Box constraints.

## Apple Ads

Inspect organization/account, storefronts, app, campaign/ad-group, search match/keywords/negative keywords, audience, schedule, CPT/budget, creative sets or current assets, taps, installs/downloads, redownload/new-user definitions, attribution/API source, MMP/SKAN, and retained/value cohorts.

App-store tap/install efficiency is not user quality. Reconcile with activation, subscription, retention, refunds, and cohort contribution.

## Programmatic display, video, audio, CTV, and retail media

Inspect:

- advertiser, DSP, seat, insertion order, line item, deal, supply source, publisher/app/site, exchange, and creative IDs;
- gross media, working media, platform/data/verification/agency/tech fees, and currency;
- bid strategy, pacing curve, frequency policy, audience/data provenance, geo/device/inventory, private-marketplace terms, and makegoods;
- ads.txt/app-ads.txt, sellers.json/supply-chain transparency where applicable, supply-path optimization, invalid traffic/fraud, brand safety/suitability, viewability/audibility, completion, reach, and duplication;
- click/view attribution, household/device identity, clean-room or privacy limitations, advertiser-owned outcomes, holdouts/geo tests, and incrementality;
- creative specs, VAST/current standards, consent strings, regional policy, and log-level data rights.

Cheap CPM is not efficient if inventory is non-viewable, fraudulent, duplicative, unsafe, or non-incremental. Viewability is an opportunity-to-see diagnostic, not proof of attention or outcome.

## Channel onboarding checklist

For any platform not listed:

1. locate current official product, measurement, policy, API, and reporting documentation;
2. map hierarchy, identifiers, money fields, outcome fields, attribution, lag, and change/audit support;
3. verify account-specific eligibility and exact delivered state;
4. connect platform outcomes to advertiser-owned business truth;
5. identify experimental/incrementality options;
6. define a minimal reversible execution/read-back contract;
7. record unresolved risks rather than importing rules from another channel.

Use references/runtime-source-registry.md and references/source-ledger.md for sources and staleness rules.
