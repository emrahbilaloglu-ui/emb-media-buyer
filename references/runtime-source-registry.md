# Runtime source registry

Platform interfaces, fields, thresholds, eligibility, reporting semantics, privacy rules, and automation products change. Verify volatile facts at runtime and record source plus retrieval time.

## Source order

For platform behavior use, in order:

1. current account/API state and change/audit history;
2. current first-party developer/help/policy documentation;
3. current official training, release notes, or engineering publications;
4. transparent research/standards;
5. practitioner/video/vendor/open-source material as hypotheses or workflow patterns.

Use advertiser-owned finance, commerce, CRM, product, and cohort records for business truth. Official platform documentation does not establish the advertiser’s unit economics or incrementality.

## Facts that always require a live check

- product/campaign/feature names and availability;
- field names, enums, defaults, API versions, limits, and deprecations;
- attribution models/windows and breakdown availability;
- learning-phase guidance, eligibility, event volume, or duration;
- budget/bid edit effects and recommended change sizes;
- experiment/lift eligibility, sample feasibility, cells, and confidence display;
- automated audience/placement/creative controls and what is a hard constraint;
- creative specs, aspect ratios, duration, text, safe area, and export requirements;
- consent, privacy, targeting, disclosure, political/special-category, and regional policy;
- modeled data, identity, offline conversion, and deduplication behavior;
- certification, policy, billing, and account-level restrictions.

Do not quote a remembered number. Open the exact current official page or read the account.

Do not say “Privacy Sandbox is retired” as a single undifferentiated fact. Google’s 2026 status page marks many advertising technologies—including Attribution Reporting, Protected Audience, Topics, and related components—for deprecation/removal while continuing support for CHIPS, FedCM, Private State Tokens, storage/network partitioning, and other privacy/security features. Verify the individual technology and platform status.

## Research and open implementations

Use research for method design, not account facts. Relevant families include randomized field experiments, geo experiments, synthetic controls, causal time series, incrementality, MMM, paid-search bias, auction/pacing systems, privacy measurement, and ad standards.

Use open implementations to inspect assumptions and reproduce calculations. Check current maintenance, license, version, examples, diagnostics, and applicability. Do not copy code or thresholds from a repository merely because it is public.

The reviewed research index, GitHub projects, official pages, and practitioner videos are in references/source-ledger.md.

## Source capture

For each externally used claim record:

- title, publisher/author, URL or account artifact ID;
- published/updated date if available;
- retrieved_at with time zone;
- exact claim supported;
- evidence class and whether first-party, academic, vendor, or practitioner;
- market/product/account scope;
- material caveat, conflict, or staleness risk.

When sources disagree, compare definitions and publication dates, prefer current primary evidence, and preserve the conflict if it is not resolvable.
