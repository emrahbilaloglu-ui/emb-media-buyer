# Creative system

Treat creative as a research, production, delivery, and learning system. A high volume of cosmetic variants is not diversification, and an ad name is not proof of what was delivered.

## Inputs before ideation

Establish:

- product/service truth, offer terms, price, availability, market, and destination;
- approved claims, qualifiers, evidence, rights, and prohibited statements;
- objective, funnel stage, audience/job, category demand, and customer language;
- past concepts and actual delivered assets, not only campaign/ad names;
- downstream performance, returns/refunds, lead quality, complaints, reviews, sales calls, and merchandising context;
- format/placement scope and current platform requirements.

Public competitor ads and creator content are hypothesis inputs. Longevity is not performance evidence; popularity is not business evidence. Extract the customer problem or proposition, never copy distinctive expression.

## Creative taxonomy

Track each creative at separable layers:

1. **Concept:** the core customer problem, desired progress, or mechanism.
2. **Audience/job:** the person and situation addressed.
3. **Angle:** the proposition made to that person now.
4. **Proof:** demonstration, specification, comparison, credential, process, or documented customer evidence.
5. **Offer:** price/value/urgency structure and its verified terms.
6. **Narrative:** problem-first, outcome-first, mechanism-first, contrast, demonstration, story, objection handling.
7. **Hook:** the opening device; it is not the whole concept.
8. **Format/creator:** static, video, carousel, catalog, search text, creator type, visual register, duration.
9. **Execution:** copy, shot, edit, crop, sound, caption, thumbnail, and design treatment.
10. **Destination/message match:** what the landing experience must confirm.

Record which layer changes in each variant. A different crop, color grade, font, or word order is cosmetic unless that surface is the explicit test variable. Two variants that would fail for the same underlying reason are not independent concepts.

## Concept record

Each concept should carry:

- immutable concept and asset identifiers;
- evidence source and truth label for the angle;
- audience/job, funnel stage, primary message, proof, and offer;
- claims and approvals;
- format/placement job and rationale;
- destination and required message match;
- hypothesis, mechanism, variation axis, primary metric, and guardrails;
- source master, derivatives, ad/asset bindings, and delivery state;
- rights, consent, product fidelity, brand, policy, and accessibility QA;
- status: idea, blocked, production-ready, produced, launched, delivering, measured, or learned.

Do not count a blocked concept as production-ready. If a claim, right, destination, product fact, or source asset is pending, list the gate and deliver a safe replacement when the user requested a fixed number.

## Portfolio coverage

Evaluate a creative portfolio across customer motivations, awareness/funnel jobs, proof types, formats, creators/visual registers, products/offers, destinations, markets/languages, and net-new concepts versus iterations.

Official Meta guidance and practitioner transcripts support the hypothesis that concept diversity matters more than merely resizing or cosmetically editing the same idea. Treat this as a portfolio design principle, then validate the actual account response. Do not impose a universal number of concepts, ads, hooks, days, or spend per test.

## Binding and delivery verification

Maintain lineage:

research/brief → generation or shoot job → master asset → derivative → platform asset/creative → ad → ad group/set → campaign → delivered placement and destination

Verify automated combinations, dynamic fields, catalog products, crops, text transformations, generated variants, and final URLs. If delivery reporting exists only at the ad or asset-group level, do not assign outcomes to a specific hook or frame.

Count ad bindings, unique platform creative IDs, unique source/render hashes, and distinct concepts separately. Ads that share a creative ID, source asset, or byte-identical rendered output are not independent creative observations even when both bindings should remain visible.

Validate retrieved media by detected type, dimensions, duration where relevant, byte size, and cryptographic hash. A successful request can still return a thumbnail, error document, signed wrapper, placeholder, or repeated stub; none is proof of the source asset. Do not use a universal byte-size cutoff—inspect suspicious clusters and triangulate against rendered previews and platform bindings.

For large visual inventories, use progressive inspection: inventory and contact sheet first, then a reduced-size complete scan, then full resolution for product identity, claims, offer terms, destinations, rights, or ambiguity. Record coverage and unresolved items; a contact sheet alone does not prove row-level binding.

## Test architecture

Choose structure from business effect, budget, outcome density, conversion lag, creative supply, and platform capability—not from a creator’s fixed recipe.

- Use observational reads for triage and hypothesis generation.
- Use a randomized A/B design for a directional comparison when eligible.
- Use holdout or geo designs for incremental outcome claims.
- Keep one learning variable when diagnostic value matters.
- Label a full creative/offer/page bundle test non-diagnostic.
- Preregister the primary metric, denominator, guardrails, sufficiency method, lag window, and decision rule.
- Report per-arm delivery and actual asset binding.

Clicks, engagement, view metrics, and platform attention diagnostics locate funnel breaks; they do not replace qualified outcomes or contribution. Compare retention only on compatible duration/format definitions.

## Fatigue is a hypothesis set

Do not declare fatigue from frequency, age, declining CTR, or a fixed impression count alone. Generate rivals:

- audience saturation or reduced newly reached share;
- short-horizon novelty decay;
- placement/device/product/audience mix shift;
- higher auction cost with stable response;
- offer, price, promotion, stock, page, or checkout change;
- tracking, consent, attribution, or lag change;
- calendar/category demand movement;
- spend concentration or regression from an early spike.

Use account history to derive an account-specific decay pattern only when assets, delivery, outcomes, and comparable conditions are verified. Label it an observation until a design supports more.

## Research-to-production loop

Operate the loop:

business/customer evidence → concept map → approved brief → production → fidelity/claims/rights QA → platform export verification → launch binding → delivery/outcome read → learning ledger → next concept map

The media buyer owns the commercial hypothesis, test design, delivery context, and outcome read. A creative strategist/producer owns executable expression. Keep a shared taxonomy so results feed new briefs rather than only ad-level winners.

For finished creative production, use a dedicated evidence-led creative skill when available, such as emb-meta-creative for Meta-specific work. Hand it product truths, approved claims, concept records, format jobs, platform/runtime requirements, destination match, and measurement plan. Do not ask generation tools to invent product geometry, testimonials, savings, results, or legal permissions.
